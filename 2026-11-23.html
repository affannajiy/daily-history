1971 · New York City, United States

People's Republic of China Attends United Nations

Representatives of the People's Republic of China attended the United Nations and its Security Council for the first time in 1971.

What happened

In 1971, representatives of the People's Republic of China attended the United Nations, including the United Nations Security Council, for the first time. The United Nations, headquartered in New York City, was established by the signing of the Charter of the United Nations on 26 June 1945 to maintain international peace and security and foster international cooperation.

The Security Council is one of six principal organs of the United Nations, alongside the General Assembly, the Economic and Social Council, the International Court of Justice, the Secretariat, and the Trusteeship Council.

What changed after

The United Nations grew to comprise 193 member states, representing nearly all sovereign states, along with two observer states, the Holy See and the State of Palestine. The organization currently operates 11 peacekeeping missions across regions including the Middle East, Kashmir, Cyprus, and South Sudan.

1946 · Haiphong, Vietnam

French Naval Bombardment of Haiphong

French naval forces bombarded the coastal city of Haiphong in 1946 following resistance from Vietnamese nationalists after World War II.

Who
French naval and infantry forces

Where
Haiphong, Vietnam

What happened

Following the defeat of Japan in World War II, Vietnamese nationalists agitated for independence against the return of French colonial forces. When French forces landed in Haiphong, they encountered resistance that resulted in the deaths of three French soldiers.

In retaliation, French naval ships, including the cruiser Suffren, shelled Haiphong, setting the city ablaze. French infantry forces commanded by Jean-Étienne Valluy subsequently entered the city, engaging in house-to-house combat supported by armored units and airplanes.

What changed after

The bombardment set Haiphong ablaze and precipitated the First Indochina War between French forces and Vietnamese nationalists. Decades later, during the Vietnam War, U.S. naval and air strikes targeted the city, including an operation on 8 May 1972 when U.S. Admiral Thomas H. Moorer ordered the mining of Haiphong harbor.

2019 · Sabah, Malaysia

Extinction of the Sumatran Rhinoceros in Malaysia

Malaysia lost its last surviving Sumatran rhinoceros when the final remaining individual died in November 2019.

Who
Imam, the last Sumatran rhinoceros
What
Died, marking species extinction in Malaysia
Where
Malaysia
When
November 2019
What happened

In 2019, the Sumatran rhinoceros became officially extinct in Malaysia following the deaths of the country's last remaining individuals. The last known bull died in May 2019, and the last cow, named Imam, died in November 2019.

The decline of the species in Malaysia was driven by forest clearing, which destroyed their natural habitats and prevented rhinos from finding mates. Poaching for rhino horns, falsely believed to possess medicinal properties, further reduced their numbers.

Timeline

November 2019 The last female Sumatran rhinoceros in Malaysia, Imam, dies, rendering the species extinct in the country.
What changed after

With the death of Imam in November 2019, the Sumatran rhinoceros was officially extirpated in Malaysia. Wild populations of fewer than 80 mature individuals remained concentrated primarily in four areas in Sumatra and one location in Borneo.
