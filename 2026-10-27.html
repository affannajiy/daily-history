Vision of the Cross

On the eve of battle, Roman Emperor Constantine reported seeing a heavenly sign that prompted his soldiers to paint Christian emblems on their shields.

What happened

In the spring of 312, Constantine gathered an army of around 40,000 soldiers to oust his brother-in-law Maxentius from Rome. Constantine overran northern Italy after winning battles near Turin and at Verona, where Maxentius' senior general Ruricius Pompeianus was killed. As Constantine's forces reached Rome, both armies prepared for battle outside the city near the Milvian Bridge over the Tiber.

On the evening of 27 October 312, Constantine experienced a vision that led him to fight under the protection of the Christian God. According to Lactantius, Constantine was commanded in a dream to mark his soldiers' shields with a sign denoting Christ, described as a staurogram. Eusebius of Caesarea recorded that Constantine saw a cross of light above the sun with the Greek words "En toutōi níka", followed by a dream in which Christ instructed him to use the sign against his enemies.

What changed after

Constantine won the battle on 28 October 312, ending the Tetrarchy and becoming the sole ruler of the Roman Empire. During the battle, Maxentius drowned in the Tiber River. His body was taken from the river, decapitated, and his head was paraded through the streets of Rome on 29 October 312 before being sent to Africa.

Following the victory, the Arch of Constantine was erected in Rome to celebrate the outcome, attributing Constantine's success to divine intervention without using overtly Christian symbols. Constantine later used the Chi-Rho sign on his military standard, the labarum, during his wars against Licinius.

Kiefer Ravena

Born on this day, 1993

Filipino point guard Kiefer Ravena earned multiple collegiate championships and national MVP awards before transitioning to play professionally in Japan.

What they did

Kiefer Isaac Crisologo Ravena was born on October 27, 1993, in Iloilo to former PBA player Bong Ravena and former volleyball player Mozzy Crisologo-Ravena. He attended Ateneo de Manila High School and joined the Ateneo Blue Eaglets in the UAAP juniors division. Ravena led the Blue Eaglets to three consecutive championships between 2008 and 2010 against the FEU Baby Tamaraws, DLSZ Junior Archers, and UST Tiger Cubs. During this period, he won two consecutive UAAP Juniors Finals MVP awards in 2009 and 2010.

Ravena joined the Ateneo Blue Eagles men's basketball team for college, playing from 2011 to 2015 while pursuing a degree in Communications Technology Management. He led the Blue Eagles to UAAP Seniors championships in 2011 and 2012, as well as four Final Four appearances. Ravena received the UAAP Season 74 Rookie of the Year award in 2011 and won back-to-back UAAP Most Valuable Player awards for Season 77 and Season 78. Internationally, he won gold medals with Gilas Pilipinas at the Southeast Asian Games in 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, and 2019, before joining the Ryukyu Golden Kings in the Japanese B.League.
