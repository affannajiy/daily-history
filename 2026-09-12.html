Battle of Marathon

An Athenian force defeated an invading Persian army at Marathon, halting King Darius's attempt to subjugate Attica.

User:Bibi Saint-Pol — CC BY-SA 3.0 · source
Who
Athenians and Plataean allies
What
Defeated the first Persian invasion force
Where
Marathon, Attica, Greece
When
490 BC
What happened

King Darius the Great ordered the second Persian campaign of the invasion of Greece in 490 BC to punish Athens and Eretria for supporting the earlier Ionian Revolt and to expand into Southeast Europe. Led by Persian commanders Datis and Artaphernes, the expedition captured and burned Naxos before leapfrogging across the Cycladic Islands. Reaching Eretria, the Persian forces besieged and captured the city, enslaving its residents and burning the town before landing at Marathon in Attica on their path toward Athens.

At Marathon, the Persian expedition was met by a smaller Athenian army joined by Plataean allies. Despite being outnumbered, the Athenian-led force secured a victory on the plain of Marathon, preventing the Persian forces from advancing onto Athens.

What changed after

The retreat of the Persian expedition to West Asia left Athens independent, though the Persian Empire retained control over Macedon, Naxos, and the Cycladic Islands.

Darius initiated preparations for a much larger invasion force to conquer all of Greece, but internal division and his death delayed the effort until his son Xerxes I launched a new invasion in 480 BC.

2007 Bengkulu earthquakes

Megathrust earthquakes striking off the coast of Sumatra generated regional tsunami alerts, killed 25 people, and injured 161 others.

Who
Indonesian emergency services and military forces
What
Responded to megathrust earthquakes and tsunamis
Where
Bengkulu, Sumatra, Indonesia
When
September 12, 2007, 18:10 local time
What happened

On September 12, 2007, at 18:10 local time (11:10 UTC), a magnitude 8.4 megathrust earthquake occurred along the Sunda Trench about 130 km southwest of Bengkulu, Sumatra, at a focal depth of 34 km. Violent tremors lasting several minutes shook Java and Sumatra, causing high-rise buildings in Jakarta to sway and crumble structures in Bengkulu. The main shock cut electrical power and communication lines across Bengkulu Province, while triggering a tsunami that reached 1 metre at Padang and 1.5 metres at Sikakap beach on South Pagai Island.

A second major earthquake measuring 7.9 magnitude struck at 23:49 UTC later that night (06:49 local time on September 13), centered 185 km south-southeast of Padang. A third severe aftershock of magnitude 7.0 followed at 10:35 local time on September 13. The series of quakes and sea surges flooded over 300 homes and public buildings on Pagai Island, resulting in 25 deaths and 161 injuries.

What changed after

The Indonesian National Armed Forces deployed to Bengkulu and the Mentawai Islands to clear collapsed structures and assist residents after tremors damaged public infrastructure and caused power outages across the region.

Tsunami alerts issued by the Pacific Tsunami Warning Centre for India, Malaysia, Thailand, and the Cocos Islands were canceled within hours after observed waves measured between 15 centimeters and 3.5 meters along coastal lines.
