2001 · Cupertino, United States

Apple Computer releases the iPod

Apple Computer introduces a portable media player that eventually becomes the longest-running discontinued product in company history.

What happened

Apple Computer designed and marketed a series of portable media players and multi-purpose mobile devices known as the iPod. The first version of the device was released on November 10, 2001, exactly 10 months after the Macintosh version of iTunes was released to the public.

Some versions of the device were built to serve as external data storage devices. Prior to macOS 10.15, Apple's iTunes software and alternative programs facilitated the transfer of music, photos, videos, games, contact information, e-mail settings, Web bookmarks, and calendars from computers running Apple macOS and Microsoft Windows operating systems.

What changed after

Apple sold an estimated 450 million iPods over the lifespan of the product line. During the middle of 2010, iPhone sales surpassed those of the iPod. Apple officially discontinued the iPod product line on May 10, 2022, marking a total lifespan of over 20 years as the longest-running discontinued product by Apple.

Signing of the Paris Peace Accords

What happened

Nineteen countries signed the Comprehensive Cambodian Peace Agreements, officially known as the Paris Peace Agreements, on 23 October 1991. The treaty marked the official end of the Cambodian-Vietnamese War and the Third Indochina War.

The agreements comprised the Final Act of the Paris Conference on Cambodia, the Agreement on the Political Settlement of the Cambodia Conflict, the Agreement Concerning the Sovereignty, Territorial Integrity and Inviolability, Neutrality and National Unity of Cambodia, and the Declaration on the Rehabilitation and Reconstruction of Cambodia.

What changed after

The agreement led directly to the deployment of the United Nations Transitional Authority in Cambodia, marking the first UN peacekeeping mission since the Cold War. It also created the first occasion in which the United Nations took over as the government of a state.

Death of Marco Simoncelli at the Malaysian Grand Prix

Italian professional motorcycle racer Marco Simoncelli dies following a racing accident during the MotoGP event in Sepang.

What happened

Marco Simoncelli competed in the MotoGP World Championship for 10 years starting from 2002. After winning the 2008 250cc World Championship with Gilera and spending four years in the intermediate class, he stepped up to the top MotoGP class in 2010 to race with the Honda Gresini Team.

On 23 October 2011, during the Malaysian Grand Prix at Sepang, Simoncelli was involved in a fatal accident while competing in the race.
