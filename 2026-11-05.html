Arrest of Guy Fawkes

English authorities discovered Guy Fawkes guarding stockpiled explosives in an undercroft beneath the House of Lords during an early morning search.

What happened

Guy Fawkes, an English Catholic who had fought for Spain in the Eighty Years' War, joined a conspiracy led by Robert Catesby to assassinate King James I and restore a Catholic monarch. Introduced to Catesby by Thomas Wintour, Fawkes was assigned to oversee a leased undercroft beneath the House of Lords, where the conspirators accumulated barrels of gunpowder.

An anonymous letter prompted royal authorities to search Westminster Palace in the early hours of 5 November 1605. Searchers discovered Fawkes guarding the concealed gunpowder and took him into custody. Over the subsequent days, Fawkes was subjected to interrogation and torture, during which he confessed to the conspiracy to destroy the House of Lords.

What changed after

Fawkes was sentenced to be hanged, drawn, and quartered, but died of a broken neck during hanging on 31 January 1606. Following the conspiracy's collapse, the Parliament of England passed legislation establishing Guy Fawkes Night, observed annually on 5 November in the United Kingdom with bonfires, effigies, and fireworks.

Tropical Storm Thelma

Torrential rainfall from Tropical Storm Thelma triggered severe flash floods across Leyte Island, destroying thousands of homes and killing thousands in Ormoc.

Where
Ormoc, Leyte Island, Philippines

5 November 1991
What happened

Tropical Storm Thelma originated from a disturbance near the Caroline Islands on November 1, 1991, approximately 415 km north-northeast of Palau. Moving westward toward the Philippines, the system intensified into a tropical storm on November 4, attaining ten-minute sustained winds of 75 km/h and a pressure of 992 mbar before making landfall in Samar.

As the storm passed over the central islands, orographic lift generated intense downpours, culminating in 580.5 mm of rainfall over Leyte Island, mostly within three hours. Deforested terrain accelerated runoff into the Anilao–Malbasag watershed, generating massive flash floods. When the surge reached Ormoc where the Anilao and Malbasag rivers meet, water inundated the city, destroying thousands of structures and causing 4,922 fatalities in Ormoc alone.

What changed after

The flooding in Ormoc City left 4,446 homes destroyed and 22,229 damaged nationwide, with financial losses totaling $27.67 million. Damaged communications initially cut off Ormoc City for more than 24 hours. Foreign governments and agencies under the United Nations subsequently provided $5.8 million in grants and emergency supplies for Philippine disaster relief.
