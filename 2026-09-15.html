Bankruptcy of Lehman Brothers

The collapse of the major investment bank triggered a global financial panic after negotiations for emergency financing fell through.

David Shankbone — CC BY-SA 3.0 · source
Who
Lehman Brothers Holdings
What
Filed a Chapter 11 bankruptcy petition
Where
New York City, United States
When
September 15, 2008
What happened

Lehman Brothers moved into mortgage origination by acquiring Alt-A lender Aurora Loan Services in 1997 and subprime lender BNC Mortgage in 2000. By 2006, Aurora and BNC were lending nearly $50 billion per month. However, Lehman accumulated $680 billion in assets supported by only $22.5 billion in firm capital by 2008, leaving its leverage ratio at 31:1 by 2007. In August 2007, the firm shut down BNC Mortgage and eliminated 1,200 positions as subprime market conditions deteriorated.

After being notified of a pending credit downgrade, the Federal Reserve summoned several banks to negotiate reorganization financing for Lehman Brothers. When these negotiations failed, Lehman filed a Chapter 11 petition on September 15, 2008, involving more than US$600 billion in assets. The filing immediately caused a 4.5% single-day drop in the Dow Jones Industrial Average and prompted mass withdrawals from money market mutual funds.

What changed after

The bankruptcy destabilized interbank lending and prompted the Federal Reserve System and U.S. government to institute several emergency interventions to halt financial market panic. Lehman Brothers Holdings remained in liquidation until September 28, 2022.

During the liquidation process, 110,000 customers eventually received all $106 billion they were owed. General unsecured creditors received $9.4 billion, representing 41 cents on the dollar.

1974 · Phan Rang, South Vietnam

Air Vietnam Flight 706 Hijacking

A demoted South Vietnamese army officer hijacked a commercial Boeing 727 with hand grenades, leading to an explosion that killed 75 people.

Who
Le Duc Tan and two accomplices
What
Hijacked a flight with hand grenades
Where
Phan Rang Air Base, South Vietnam
When
September 15, 1974
What happened

On September 15, 1974, Air Vietnam Flight 706, a Boeing 727, departed Da Nang International Airport on a scheduled domestic flight to Saigon's Tan Son Nhat International Airport. Le Duc Tan, a South Vietnamese army ranger who had recently been demoted from captain to lieutenant for stealing two cars, bypassed security checkpoints at Da Nang. Joined by two accomplices, Tan produced two hand grenades shortly after takeoff and demanded that the flight crew redirect the aircraft to Hanoi in North Vietnam.

The pilot informed the hijackers that the Boeing 727 required a refueling stop at Phan Rang Air Base in South Vietnam. As the plane approached the air base for an emergency landing, the pilot radioed that Tan was pulling the pins on the two hand grenades. The aircraft overshot its base leg, initiated a left turn, lost control, and plunged to the ground from an altitude of 1,000 feet, killing all 67 passengers and 8 crew members on board.

1995 · Tawau, Malaysia

Malaysia Airlines Flight 2133 Crash

A domestic passenger flight overran a short airport runway in Sabah and crashed into a residential neighborhood, killing 34 people.

Who
Malaysia Airlines Flight 2133 crew
What
Overran runway and crashed into houses
Where
Tawau Airport, Sabah, Malaysia
When
15 September 1995, 13:05 (GMT+8)
What happened

On 15 September 1995, Malaysia Airlines Flight 2133, a Fokker 50 carrying 49 passengers and 4 crew members, departed Kota Kinabalu International Airport at 12:19 local time for Tawau Airport after a 30-minute departure delay. Approaching Tawau's 5,600-foot runway, the aircraft was flying too high and fast, descending at 3,000 feet per minute at a pitch angle of -13 degrees. Captain Wong Khang Lock ignored Ground Proximity Warning System alerts despite warnings from First Officer Su Tiong Hee regarding excess speed.

At 13:05 (GMT+8), the aircraft touched down 3,400 feet down the runway, bounced twice, and finally settled with only 800 feet of runway remaining. Unable to stop, the aircraft overran the runway and crashed into the Sri Menanti shanty town approximately 500 meters away, where it broke into two pieces and ignited. Firefighters deployed 18 personnel to the site, eventually extinguishing the flames that destroyed 40 houses and killed 32 passengers and 2 crew members.

What changed after

The final investigation report published in 1998 recommended that the Malaysian civil aviation regulatory body make crew resource management training mandatory for airliners in Malaysia.

Investigators also issued several recommendations to Malaysia Airlines regarding company policies on fuel-saving and punctuality that had influenced crew decision-making during landing.
