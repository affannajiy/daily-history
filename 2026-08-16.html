What happened

Nikephoros II Phokas had a successful military career before becoming emperor, conquering Cilicia and retaking the islands of Crete and Cyprus. He was appointed the military governor of the Anatolic Theme in 945 and later became Domestic of the Schools, placing him in charge of the eastern Byzantine army.

What changed after

The Byzantine Empire saw a resurgence during the 10th century, with Nikephoros II Phokas contributing to the conquest of Cilicia and the retaking of Crete and Cyprus. The empire's military campaigns expanded, reaching as far as Upper Mesopotamia and the Levant.
