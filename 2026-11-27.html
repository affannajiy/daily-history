Council of Clermont

What happened

The Council of Clermont assembled from 17 to 27 November 1095 as a mixed synod of ecclesiastics and laymen called by Pope Urban II in Clermont, Auvergne. Attended by about 300 French clerics, the synod primarily focused on implementing Cluniac reforms, enacting church decrees, extending the excommunication of Philip I of France for his adulterous remarriage to Bertrade of Montfort, and renewing the Truce of God to reduce feuding among Frankish nobles.

On the final day, 27 November 1095, Pope Urban II delivered a speech responding to envoys sent by Byzantine emperor Alexius I Comnenus to the Council of Piacenza requesting military aid against the Seljuk Turks. Urban called upon Western forces to assist eastern churches, appointing Adhemar of Le Puy to lead an expedition scheduled to set out on 15 August 1096.

What changed after

Urban's call led directly to the mobilization of Frankish forces, culminating in the capture of Jerusalem and the establishment of the Kingdom of Jerusalem. Across Gaul, soldiers responded to reports of the speech by sewing crosses onto their right shoulders to indicate their participation.

U.S. Troop Escalation in the Vietnam War

President Lyndon B. Johnson expanded American military commitments in Southeast Asia, rapidly increasing U.S. troop strength during the mid-1960s.

What happened

Lyndon B. Johnson assumed the U.S. presidency in 1963 following the assassination of John F. Kennedy and won reelection in a landslide victory in 1964. His foreign policy prioritized the containment of communism, leading to a steady increase of American military commitments in Southeast Asia throughout his term.

During Johnson's presidency, American troop levels in the Vietnam War expanded from 16,000 personnel in 1963 to 550,000 by early 1968. This rapid deployment of forces led to intense combat operations, resulting in more than 1,000 American soldiers killed per month by early 1968.

What changed after

Rising military casualties and public domestic unrest generated a credibility gap regarding official war reporting. Following a disappointing outcome in the 1968 New Hampshire primary, Johnson withdrew his re-election candidacy and retired to his Texas ranch.

What they did

Sultan Nazrin Shah was born on 27 November 1956 in George Town, Penang, as the eldest son of Sultan Azlan Shah and Tuanku Bainun. He received his education at Sekolah Rendah Jalan Kuantan, St. John's Institution, and The Leys School before earning a bachelor's degree from Worcester College, Oxford, followed by a master's degree and doctorate from Harvard University.

Proclaimed Raja Kechil Besar in 1984 and Raja Muda of Perak in 1987, he served as Regent of Perak from 1989 to 1994 and again from 2008 to 2014. He ascended the throne as the 35th Sultan of Perak on 29 May 2014 upon his father's death. He was elected Deputy Yang di-Pertuan Agong in 2016 and re-elected in 2019 and 2024.

What still stands

Sultan Nazrin Shah serves as Chancellor of Universiti Malaya and Chairman of the Board of Governors of the Malay College Kuala Kangsar. He holds military posts as Colonel-in-Chief of the Royal Army Engineers Regiment and the Royal Army Medical Corps.
