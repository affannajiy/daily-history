What happened

The British Empire comprised the dominions, colonies, protectorates, mandates, and other territories ruled or administered by the United Kingdom and its predecessor states. By 1913, the British Empire held sway over 412 million people, 23 per cent of the world population at the time. In 1947, Pakistan gained independence from the British Empire as the Dominion of Pakistan, due to the partition of India.

What changed after

After independence, many former British colonies, along with most of the dominions, joined the Commonwealth of Nations, a free association of independent states.

What they did

He became known in the region as Mahasi Sayadaw and helped establish meditation centers all over Burma as well as in Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Thailand.

What still stands

Mahasi Sayadaw's legacy includes the establishment of meditation centers in several countries and the training of over 700,000 meditators by 1972. His teachings on Vipassana meditation continue to be practiced and studied around the world.
