1789 · Kingdom of France

Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen

France's National Constituent Assembly adopted a foundational text of civil rights inspired by Enlightenment ideals as a first step toward a constitution.

What happened

The content of the document emerged from Enlightenment philosophy. Marquis de Lafayette prepared the primary draft in consultation with Thomas Jefferson. Abbé Emmanuel Joseph Sieyès and Honoré Mirabeau assisted in conceptualizing the text, with Sieyès drafting the majority of the final version.

The Assembly's sixth bureau, led by Jérôme Champion de Cicé, introduced a 24-article draft for debate among thirty conference groups. Following modifications during floor discussions, the National Constituent Assembly adopted the final article of the Declaration on August 26, 1789, establishing equal legal protection and universal human rights.

What changed after

The Declaration established a body of universal rights valid across all times and places. It created a legal framework protecting free individuals equally under national law.

France integrated the text into the preamble of the constitution of the French Fourth Republic in 1946 and the French Fifth Republic in 1958, giving it standing as constitutional law.

Prem Tinsulanonda

Born on this day, 1920

General Prem Tinsulanonda served as Prime Minister of Thailand from 1980 to 1988 and later served as Regent of Thailand in 2016.

What they did

Born in Songkhla Province, Prem entered the Royal Thai Army Academy in 1941. He joined the Constitution Drafting Committee in 1959, served as Senator from 1968 to 1971, and became a Member of Parliament in 1972. Under Prime Minister Kriangsak Chamanan, Prem served as Deputy Interior Minister from 1977 to 1978 and Minister of Defence from 1979 to 1986. Following Kriangsak's retirement in 1980, Prem was selected as Prime Minister, leading three administrations across five cabinets until 1988. He negotiated with the Communist Party of Thailand and declared an amnesty, ending the internal insurgency.

Prem defeated military coup attempts in April 1981 and September 1985. As President of the Privy Council, he became Regent of Thailand following the death of King Bhumibol Adulyadej on October 13, 2016, holding the post until Vajiralongkorn was proclaimed King on December 1, 2016. He died on May 26, 2019, at the age of 98.

What still stands

Prem established social programs for drug suppression, poverty relief, national unity, and education. He founded the Prem Center for International Education.

Nicol David

Born on this day, 1983

What they did

Born in Penang, Nicol David began playing squash at age five. She won the Women's World Junior Squash Championship in Antwerp in 1999 at age 15, becoming the youngest world junior champion, and successfully defended the title in Penang in 2001 under coach Richard Glanfield. Turning professional in 2000, she won her first WISPA title at the Savcor Finnish Open in February 2000.

David held the world number one ranking for 108 consecutive months until September 2015 and stayed in the world top 10 for 151 consecutive months through July 2016. She won eight World Open titles in 2005, 2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, and 2014, five British Open titles, nine Asian Squash Championships, and seven WSA Player of the Year awards. She retired at the end of the 2018/2019 season.
