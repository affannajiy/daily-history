1977 · Merca, Somalia

Ali Maow Maalin Smallpox Rash

Hospital worker Ali Maow Maalin developed a smallpox rash in Somalia on October 26, 1977, marking history's last natural case.

Who
Ali Maow Maalin
What
Developed rash from Variola minor smallpox
Where
Merca, Somalia
When
26 October 1977
What happened

On 12 October 1977, two children with smallpox symptoms were found at an encampment near Kurtunawarey, 90 km from Merca. Ali Maow Maalin, a 23-year-old hospital cook and guide, accompanied the children in a closed Land Cruiser to an isolation camp, spending 5 to 15 minutes inside the vehicle. On 22 October, Maalin developed fever and headache, initially receiving treatment for malaria. On 26 October 1977, a rash appeared, but hospital staff diagnosed him with chickenpox and discharged him.

Maalin walked freely through Merca Hospital and met with relatives while infected. On 30 October 1977, a nurse colleague reported him to authorities for a reward of 200 Somali shillings ($35), leading to his transfer to an isolation camp where tests confirmed Variola minor smallpox. He experienced no further complications and recovered fully by late November 1977.

What changed after

Public health officials coordinated by Jason Weisfeld and Karl Markvart identified 161 contacts, including 91 face-to-face contacts. Workers vaccinated 54,777 residents across Merca over two weeks, set up police checkpoints on town exits, and quarantined Merca Hospital.

On 17 April 1978, the WHO Nairobi office confirmed via telegram that no additional cases were found. Exactly two years after Maalin's rash appeared, on 26 October 1979, the World Health Organization formally declared the global eradication of smallpox.

Proclamation of the Republic of Vietnam

Ngô Đình Diệm established the Republic of Vietnam in Saigon on October 26, 1955, after deposing Emperor Bảo Đại in a referendum.

What happened

In June 1954, former emperor Bảo Đại appointed Ngô Đình Diệm as Prime Minister of the State of Vietnam in Saigon. Following the 1954 Geneva Conference, the government administered territories south of the 17th parallel, comprising Cochinchina and parts of Annam. Diệm scheduled a referendum in 1955 to determine the state's form of government, which resulted in the deposal of Bảo Đại.

On 26 October 1955, Ngô Đình Diệm formally declared himself president of the newly established Republic of Vietnam. He designated Saigon as the national capital and adopted personalism as the regime's official ideology.

What changed after

By January 1975, 95 nations recognized the sovereignty of the Republic of Vietnam, though a Soviet veto in 1957 blocked its admission to the United Nations. Diệm governed until his death during a CIA-backed military coup led by General Dương Văn Minh in 1963.

Communist forces captured Saigon on 30 April 1975, bringing an end to the republic. On 2 July 1976, North Vietnam merged with the Republic of South Vietnam to form the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, renaming Saigon as Ho Chi Minh City.

What they did

Guy Theodore Sebastian was born in Klang, Selangor, on 26 October 1981, before relocating to Adelaide, Australia. In 2003, he won the inaugural season of Australian Idol and released his debut single "Angels Brought Me Here," which became the highest-selling song in Australia for the decade 2000 to 2009. He subsequently released eleven top-ten albums and achieved six number-one singles on the Australian charts.

Sebastian represented Australia at the 2015 Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna with "Tonight Again," finishing in fifth place. He served as a judge on The X Factor Australia from 2010 to 2012 and from 2015 to 2016, and worked as a coach on The Voice Australia from 2019 to 2024.
