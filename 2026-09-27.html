1066 · Mouth of the Somme River, France

Norman Conquest of England Begins

Duke William built a fleet to invade England after Harold Godwinson seized the crown following King Edward's death in January 1066.

Myrabella — Public domain · source
Who
William, Duke of Normandy, and his army
What
Invaded England with a large fleet
Where
From mouth of Somme river to England
When
September 1066
What happened

Following the death of the childless King Edward the Confessor in January 1066, Earl Harold Godwinson took the English throne. Duke William of Normandy contested Harold's succession, arguing that Edward had earlier promised him the crown and that Harold had sworn to support his claim. William assembled a large fleet and a Franco-Norman army to prepare for a cross-Channel invasion.

In September 1066, William launched his invasion forces across the English Channel from Normandy. His forces landed in England and engaged the Anglo-Saxon troops under Harold Godwinson at the Battle of Hastings on 14 October 1066. William defeated and killed Harold during the confrontation, clearing his path to power in England.

What changed after

William was crowned king of England in London on Christmas Day in 1066. He subsequently suppressed English revolts, settled a new Norman nobility across the land, and altered the composition of the English clergy.

In 1086, William ordered the compilation of the Domesday Book to survey land-holdings across England. Following his death in September 1087, his territory was divided between his sons, with Robert receiving Normandy and William Rufus inheriting England.

1988 · Yangon, Myanmar

Formation of the National League for Democracy

Pro-democracy leaders formed the National League for Democracy on 27 September 1988 following the military coup that ended the 8888 Uprising.

Who

Founded the National League for Democracy

Myanmar

What happened

In the aftermath of the 8888 Uprising, a series of pro-democracy protests that concluded with a military coup, democracy advocates organized a political party in Myanmar. On 27 September 1988, they founded the National League for Democracy (NLD). Aung San Suu Kyi, daughter of independence figure Aung San, was recruited into the movement and served as the party's General Secretary, while Brigadier General Aung Gyi became its first founding chair.

The party contested the 1990 parliamentary elections, securing 59% of the vote and winning 392 out of 492 contested seats. Despite this victory, the ruling military junta refused to permit the party to form a government, repressing NLD members and placing Aung San Suu Kyi under house arrest in 1996.

What changed after

The military junta blocked the NLD from forming a government after the 1990 vote, prompting escaped party members to form the National Coalition Government of the Union of Burma. Aung San Suu Kyi was held under house arrest for 16 of the next 21 years until her release on 13 November 2010.

The party later won 43 out of 44 contested seats in the 2012 by-elections and secured a supermajority in the 2015 general election, bringing to power Myanmar's first non-military president in 54 years.

1977 · Subang, Malaysia

Crash of Japan Airlines Flight 715

A Japan Air Lines DC-8 crashed into a hill near Elmina Estate on 27 September 1977 during a low approach in bad weather.

Who
Japan Air Lines flight crew
What
Crashed DC-8 aircraft during airport approach
Where
Elmina Estate near Subang Airport, Malaysia
When
27 September 1977
What happened

On 27 September 1977, Japan Air Lines Flight 715 was en route from Haneda Airport in Tokyo to Singapore, with scheduled stopovers in Hong Kong and Subang Airport in Malaysia. Two hours into the flight, air traffic control directed the McDonnell Douglas DC-8 to begin its approach to runway 15 at Subang Airport. The flight crew lowered the landing gear and extended the flaps amidst bad weather.

During the VOR approach, the captain continued descending below the minimum descent altitude of 750 feet without visual contact with the runway. At 300 feet, the aircraft struck the side of a hill approximately 4 miles from the airport near Elmina Estate. The impact broke the aircraft apart and triggered a fire that airport rescue teams later extinguished.

Timeline

27 September 1977 Flight 715 crashes into a hill near Elmina Estate while approaching Subang Airport.
December 1977 Malaysian Airline System Flight 653 crashes three months later with 100 fatalities.
What changed after

The Malaysian Department of Civil Aviation investigated the crash and determined that the captain's descent below the minimum altitude in bad weather without seeing the runway caused the accident, compounded by the first officer's failure to challenge the flight violation.

Rescue workers transported 45 injured survivors to the hospital, while 34 people died in the crash. A memorial dedicated to the victims was built at the Japanese cemetery in Kuala Lumpur.
