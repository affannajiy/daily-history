Assassination of John F. Kennedy

United States President John F. Kennedy was assassinated in Dallas on November 22, 1963, leading Vice President Lyndon B. Johnson to assume the presidency.

What happened

On November 22, 1963, 35th U.S. President John F. Kennedy was assassinated while visiting Dallas. Following the shooting, his vice president, Lyndon B. Johnson, assumed the presidency of the United States.

Lee Harvey Oswald was arrested in connection with the assassination. Two days after his arrest, Oswald was shot and killed by Jack Ruby while in custody.

What changed after

Following Kennedy's death, the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Warren Commission investigated the assassination and concluded that Lee Harvey Oswald acted alone.

Congress subsequently enacted major legislative proposals originating from Kennedy's administration, passing the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and the Revenue Act of 1964.

What happened

Bon Om Touk, also known as the Cambodian Water and Moon Festival, is celebrated in late October or early November in Phnom Penh to mark the end of the monsoon season and the changing flow of the Tonlé Sap River. The multi-day festival attracts millions of visitors annually for boat races along the Sisowath Quay riverfront, evening concerts, and fireworks displays.

During the festivities, participants take part in the Royal Boat Race, release illuminated floats during the Bondet Bratib ceremony, and perform the Og Ambok ceremony at midnight to worship the Moon.

What changed after

Following various incidents, natural disasters, and the COVID-19 pandemic, Cambodian authorities frequently suspended the annual Water Festival celebrations in Phnom Penh.

What they did

Tunku Ibrahim Ismail was born on 22 November 1958 at Sultanah Aminah Hospital in Johor Bahru. He was educated at Sekolah Temenggong Abdul Rahman 1, Trinity Grammar School in Sydney, and Maktab Sultan Abu Bakar, and received religious education at Sekolah Agama Bukit Zaharah and Sekolah Agama Air Molek. He completed military training at PULADA in Kota Tinggi, Fort Benning, and Fort Bragg. On 3 July 1981, he was appointed Crown Prince of Johor, later serving as Regent of Johor from 1984 to 1989.

Ibrahim succeeded his father as Sultan of Johor in January 2010. During his reign, he declared Muar as the royal capital of Johor in 2012 and changed the state's official weekend to Friday and Saturday starting in 2014. On 27 October 2023, the Conference of Rulers elected him as Yang di-Pertuan Agong, and he was officially sworn in as the 17th King of Malaysia on 31 January 2024.
